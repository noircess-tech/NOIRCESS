FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Pricing questions, answered.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. They are starting prices. Final quotations depend on the scope, complexity and functionality required.

Do your prices include VAT?

Published prices exclude applicable VAT. Any applicable VAT is added to the final quotation.

How much deposit is required?

Our standard deposit is 50% for most projects. Large and complex engagements may have negotiated milestone arrangements.

Is maintenance included?

Project packages include maintenance for an agreed period and scope. Additional ongoing maintenance can be arranged separately.

Can I request a discount?

Discounts are considered individually based on project scope, commercial viability and the nature of the engagement.

Do you accept international clients?

Yes. International quotations can be issued in USD, while KES remains our primary pricing currency.

Do these prices cover NOIRCESS products?

No. STOXA, ZENTRO, FINO and HAULO have separate product pricing arrangements.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes. We provide combined service engagements with individually scoped quotations.
