Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about working with NOIRCESS, our services and project enquiries.

Still have questions?
What services does NOIRCESS offer?

NOIRCESS provides software development, digital transformation, creative services, technology advisory and IT support. Our solutions cover different business and technology requirements.

How do I request a quotation?

Submit an enquiry through our contact form or email us with your project requirements. We will use the information to understand your needs and prepare an appropriate quotation.

Does NOIRCESS work with startups and SMEs?

Yes. NOIRCESS works with startups, small and medium-sized businesses, and enterprises. Solutions are considered according to the client's requirements, objectives and available budget.

Can I request a custom software solution?

Yes. We provide custom software development for organisations seeking technology solutions designed around their operational and business requirements.

How long does a project take?

Project timelines depend on the scope, complexity, requirements and agreed deliverables. A timeline is established during project assessment and planning.

Does NOIRCESS work with international clients?

Yes. We can discuss project requirements with clients outside Kenya and explore suitable remote collaboration arrangements.
